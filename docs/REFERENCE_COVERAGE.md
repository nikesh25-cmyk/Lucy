# World Assembly reference coverage

The independent reference desk now contains 83 records. Sixteen additions distinguish ten human rights treaty bodies, the Human Rights Council, Universal Periodic Review, Special Procedures and three UNFCCC constituted bodies: the Least Developed Countries Expert Group, Standing Committee on Finance and Technology Executive Committee. Bodies, review mechanisms and specialized agencies retain separate categories. NemoWorld has no UN affiliation or mandate.

The treaty-body list and Charter/expert distinctions were checked against the official [OHCHR Bangkok overview](https://bangkok.ohchr.org/un-mechanisms) and [OHCHR Europe overview](https://europe.ohchr.org/human-rights/human-rights-mechanism). The [Treaty Body Database](https://tbinternet.ohchr.org/_layouts/15/TreatyBodyExternal/Home.aspx) supplies country-document and participation discovery routes. The [Universal Human Rights Index description](https://uhri.ohchr.org/en/about) explains review mechanisms and its coverage limits. Main OHCHR overview requests returned 403, so regional references are identified explicitly. No specific country participation, complaint eligibility, procedural deadline or compliance conclusion is established by the catalog.

Climate references were checked against official [LEG](https://unfccc.int/LEG), [SCF](https://unfccc.int/SCF) and [TEC](https://unfccc.int/ttclear/tec) pages. Adaptation Committee page retrieval timed out and that body is not part of this addition. No committee reference constitutes project financing or a country support commitment.

Each new record retains a check date, source location, editorial research suggestions, proposed SDG mappings and required country evidence checks. Related official discovery URLs become proposed task sources and approved hosts only through the existing human activation workflow. Suggestions and checks are NemoWorld’s task design, not instructions issued by those bodies. Generated tasks preserve source dates and applicability checks, and distinguish recommendations from implementation or financing needs from actual disbursement. Country-baseline and implementation-brief proposals still require named beneficiaries, owners, independent reviewers and original country evidence.

Existing country proposals, activated task scopes, accounts and world data are preserved. Added references do not automatically activate missions or create research submissions. Coverage remains partial for subsidiary bodies, individual Special Procedures mandates, climate bodies, treaty secretariats, funding mechanisms and programme-level agendas. These limits are visible in the reference desk.

Verification: 172 tests passed at that milestone. Tests exercise the new categories, distinct Council/Committee names, deduplicated country proposals, discovery routes, preserved country checks after activation, and zero automatic submissions/adoptions. Actual isolated browser QA displayed all ten treaty bodies and their evidence requirements, then a reviewer generated two Nepal disability-rights proposals. Those proposals are workflow fixtures, not completed studies or government recommendations.


The 12 October expansion adds four peacebuilding, food-security and disaster-risk references; see `STABILITY_REFERENCES.md` for secured retrieval proof, country checks and verification. The earlier sixteen-record addition and its 172-test milestone above are historical.


Five climate implementation/resilience references were added on 12 October; see `CLIMATE_REFERENCES.md`. The catalog now has seven climate constituted bodies and one technical-assistance network. Direct retrieval gaps are visible and country requirements remain task-specific.


Country coordination and SDG review references now extend the desk to 83 records; see `COUNTRY_COORDINATION.md`. Country-specific reviews, current office roles and framework cycles remain unverified until scoped research is completed.
