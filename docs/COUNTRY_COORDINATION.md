# Country coordination and review references

Four additions bring the institutional/mechanism desk to 83 references: the High-level Political Forum, Voluntary National Reviews, the Resident Coordinator system and country Cooperation Frameworks. They remain distinct types: forum, review mechanism, coordination system and programming framework. NemoWorld is independent and has no UN affiliation or country mandate.

## Sources and qualifications

- [UN monitoring and HLPF overview](https://www.un.org/sustainabledevelopment/monitoring-and-progress-hlpf/) was read with the secured DNS-pinned reader. It supports the forum and VNR roles; retrieval date/hash/size are retained in `coordination-source-verification.json`. It does not verify particular country reports or outcomes.
- [Resident Coordinator role](https://unsdg.un.org/2030-agenda/leadership/the-resident-coordinator) and [Cooperation Framework overview](https://unsdg.un.org/2030-agenda/cooperation-framework) were reviewed through official indexed text. Direct retrieval remained unavailable or unsupported; no successful live mandate retrieval or response hash is claimed for those records.
- The [2030 Agenda](https://sdgs.un.org/2030agenda) supplies follow-up/review context. The discovered [2026 VNR handbook](https://hlpf.un.org/sites/default/files/2025-10/VNR%202026%20Handbook%20EN.pdf) is a dated discovery route requiring separate full review; it is not registered for automatic PDF reading or assumed to govern the next review cycle.

Each record has editorial task suggestions, explicit source-check notes and country evidence requirements. Generated proposals and activated tasks preserve them. National reviews are country-led/self-reported documents, not independent certification. A current country Cooperation Framework, its programme period/amendments, national priorities and results reports must be located rather than inferred from generic guidance. Current office roles and public access routes must be checked; fictional mayors do not become Resident Coordinators and a public route is not consent to engagement. Plans, financing needs and evidenced outcomes stay separate.

Past forum dates and handbook years are not new submission deadlines. These references do not offer project finance, recipient agreement, country eligibility or authority to contact an institution. Adding the catalog records does not generate or activate proposals in the saved world.
