# Entry navigation

NemoWorld now connects its world, country ambassador roster, country evidence and research backlog through three entry paths. The Guide explains activation, contribution, independent review and recorded use. The home hero offers research and character actions before and after sign-in. Counts come from existing public catalogs and describe proposals, not completed studies. A scrollable desktop sidebar preserves access to longer signed-in navigation.

Visitor browser QA followed the pre-migration mayor, country and research routes; that mayor framing was later replaced by the per-country ambassadors (see [the task 01 handoff](handoff/task-01-country-ambassadors.md)). Signed-in coin wallet QA verified the extended sidebar route. Account creation remains with the user; no production identity was created during QA.
