# Entry navigation

Lucy now connects its world, mayor roster, country evidence and research backlog through three entry paths. The Guide explains activation, contribution, independent review and recorded use. The home hero offers research and character actions before and after sign-in. Counts come from existing public catalogs and describe proposals, not completed studies. A scrollable desktop sidebar preserves access to longer signed-in navigation.

Visitor browser QA followed mayor, country and research routes. Signed-in coin wallet QA verified the extended sidebar route. Account creation remains with the user; no production identity was created during QA.
