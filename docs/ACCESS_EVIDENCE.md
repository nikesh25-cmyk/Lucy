# Electricity and Internet evidence

NemoWorld now retains two national annual percentage series for all 44 LDC directory entries plus India: 90 series and 990 annual records in the fixed 2015–2026 request window. Current responses have eleven rows (2015–2025); requested 2026 is not a reported observation. Missing values remain null and are not zero. Five initial request timeouts succeeded on a single bounded retry. Retained series are not evidence of completed studies or universal country coverage.

## Sources and interpretation

- Electricity access: World Bank SDG 7.1.1 Electrification Dataset, distributed through WDI. Definition and CC BY 4.0 checked 12 October 2026: https://databank.worldbank.org/metadataglossary/world-development-indicators/series/EG.ELC.ACCS.ZS
- Individuals using the Internet: ITU, distributed through World Bank WDI. Measures use from any location within the previous three months. Definition, CC BY 4.0 and explicit ITU attribution checked 12 October 2026: https://databank.worldbank.org/metadataglossary/world-development-indicators/series/IT.NET.USER.ZS

National access percentages do not establish reliable/affordable electricity, generation capacity, broadband coverage, speed, skills or a local project design. Observation year, dataset update and retrieval date are displayed separately. Provider country notes and footnotes require further review. No averages, forecasts, rankings or completion scores are inferred.

Every retained response has original JSON bytes, SHA256, canonical fixed API URL, retrieval timestamp, country and indicator identity, percentage units and provider qualifiers. The retained data are in catalog/access-statistics.json; hashed original responses are in catalog/statistics/. ITU attribution is included in normalized source records and the UI.

## Platform integration

Countries and atlas selection offer Electricity & internet evidence. The evidence view displays dated cards, full annual records, provenance, methodology links and an access-evidence JSON download. Its buttons open country-scoped SDG 7 or 17 research. The whole-country programme also includes access evidence coverage and a navigation route.

Unactivated general SDG 7 and 17 baseline proposals receive their respective registered statistical source and qualifications. Previously activated tasks and approved scopes are preserved. No new backlog, account, claim, submission, adoption or external contact is created by this installation. Agriculture routes, evidence fields, coverage colours and default download remain compatible.

The secured reader now recognizes exactly five registered percentage indicators. Curator collection accepts only unique registered indicator identifiers; default collection remains the three agriculture indicators. Partial refresh preserves failures for indicators not requested. Network source restrictions, identity/date/percentage validation, byte limits, deadlines and human review stay in force.
