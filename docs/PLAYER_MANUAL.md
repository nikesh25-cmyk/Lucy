# Harr: a player's manual

Welcome. Harr is a quiet research village inside the Veloren game. People and AI residents live there together:
scholars, farmers, builders, makers and couriers. You come to learn, help build, share a meal and discover
places in the valley. There is nothing to win, no one to beat, and nothing to fear. Take it at your own pace.

This manual is for someone who has never been before.

## 1. Getting in

How you join depends on who runs your Harr. Today a Harr runs on its host's own computer, so you need an invitation.

1. **Ask your host for three things:** the game address to connect to, an invitation to the NemoWorld website
   (a single-use link), and the name of your Harr.
2. **Make a NemoWorld account** from the invitation. This is where your progress is kept safely. Nobody else can
   read your private journal, and nothing in the game can read it aloud.
3. **Start the Veloren game** and add the server address you were given. Choose a name.
4. **Choose your instrument.** Character creation asks you to choose an instrument (lute, flute, lyre, kalimba or
   oud) instead of a tool. It is only for the look of your character; nobody is hurt by any of them.
5. **Link your character to your account** (one minute, once): walk up to Lyra Scrollkeeper, whisper
   `link my account`, and she sends you a private code. Type that code on the NemoWorld link page while signed in.
   Linking is optional, but without it the game cannot show your passport, calling or quests.

If anything here does not match what you see, trust your host and the game over this page.

## 2. Controls

Harr uses Veloren's normal controls. Every key can be changed in the game under Settings, then Controls.

| To do this | Press (default) |
| --- | --- |
| Walk | W A S D, mouse to look |
| Jump | Space |
| Open chat | Enter |
| Open the Harr panel | **H** |
| Close any window | Esc |

To ask Selene (the village host) something, open chat and whisper her one word:

```
/tell Selene menu
```

Selene answers privately, in a few short lines. Say `more` to read the next page of a long answer, or add a number,
for example `quests 2`. If you misspell a word she will guess what you meant.

## 3. What to do first

Do these in any order. Selene's `next` always names the single best one for you right now.

1. **Say hello.** Walk up to the people around the plaza, the workshop and the fields and stand beside them
   for a moment. Meeting five villagers is your first small task.
2. **Share a meal.** Whisper Selene `take apple`. The pantry is free for everyone and is restocked by the farms.
3. **Find a place.** Whisper Selene `library`, pick a place on the list, walk there and stay a few seconds. Every
   find adds a page to the village library and gives the whole village insight toward its next technology.

When you want more: `tasks` is a gentle checklist, `start` walks you through your first ten minutes, and
`quests` shows longer journeys.

## 4. Everything Selene knows

Whisper one word. Nothing needs typing twice and nothing can go wrong.

| Word | What you get | Needs a linked account |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `menu` | Every word below, one line each | no |
| `help` | What Harr is, your one next step, how to ask | no |
| `start` | Your first ten minutes | no (a short welcome) |
| `next` | The one best thing to do now | no (a short welcome) |
| `tasks` | A small checklist of first steps | no |
| `works` | What the builders are making and what they need | no |
| `library` | Places to discover, and which are found | no |
| `pantry` | The free food on the shelves | no |
| `today` | Market hour, story circle and festival times | no |
| `chronicle` | What happened in Harr lately | no |
| `purpose` | Why Harr exists and how far along it is | no |
| `research` | What the village is learning | no |
| `passport` | Your badges, skills and visits | yes |
| `journal` | How many entries you wrote (never the text) | yes |
| `calling` | Your calling and today's gentle objective | yes |
| `quests` | Longer journeys you can join | yes |
| `karma` | Your Karma Coins | yes |
| `community` | Gatherings and circles | yes |
| `track` | Marks your current step on your map | yes |

Other residents have a few words of their own: **Vaelen** keeps the map (`places`, `travel to` a place),
**Rowan** keeps the cottages (`build my home`, `enter`, `leave`), **Kael** keeps the shop (`what is for sale`,
`my balance`), and **Lyra** is the scholar who links your account.

Selene and the others are scripted guides. They only read and tell. They cannot give, buy, approve or change
anything for you, and they cannot see anything you did not do.

## 5. The village rhythm

Harr has a daily and weekly rhythm. Whisper `today` to see the clock.

* **Market hour** at the plaza: makers, farmers and couriers lay out what they have made. Everyone may look, ask and share.
* **Story circle** at the field pavilion: keepers and scholars read aloud what the village has learned and welcome new pages.
* **Festival** once a week in the square, to celebrate what has been built and found.
* **Building** goes stage by stage, and a stage is built only while a crew of two or more stands at the site.
  Whisper `works` to see which project needs hands.
* **Research** moves forward as the village finds places, finishes stages and holds gatherings. Say `research`.

The world moves while the server runs. If the host's computer sleeps, Harr rests with it.

## 6. The companion dashboard

Open `/harr` on the NemoWorld website (your host will give you the address). It shows the village at a glance:
a short "What can I do now?" list, recent activity, the map, goals, public works, the research tree, the pantry and
the library.

* First time: a welcome card explains the page. Press **Got it** and it will not return (press `w` to see it again).
* **Keyboard:** `j` and `k` move between sections, `c` switches to a compact view for a second monitor, `t` cycles
  light, dark and automatic colours, `r` refreshes, `?` lists every shortcut. Skip links at the top of the page jump
  to the main content, the section menu or recent activity.
* Each section shows when its information last changed. If the village cannot be reached, the page keeps what it
  last had and tells you so.
* **Printing:** the page has a plain print layout without the map and buttons.
* The compact view also opens straight from `/harr?view=compact`.

## 7. Questions

**Is there fighting, hunting or danger?** No. Harr is a peaceful world for learning, building and sharing.
Nothing can hurt you and you cannot hurt anyone.

**Is it competitive? Are there leaderboards?** No. Progress is shared. A find you make adds insight for everyone.

**Do I need to spend real money?** No. Harr never asks for real money. Karma Coins are earned only by saved service
in NemoWorld and are not money.

**Who are the AI residents?** Villagers who are computer-run and say so. They have jobs, they talk to you, and they
never speak for a government, a company or a real person.

**Selene says "Link first". What does that mean?** Your character is not linked to a NemoWorld account yet.
The village words still work; the personal ones need the link in section 1.

**Selene did not understand me.** Say `menu`. She also guesses near-miss words ("quesst" becomes `quests`).

**My progress did not move after I did something.** Progress comes from facts the game server saves, not from chat.
Give it a few seconds and ask `next` again.

**Can someone read my journal?** No. It is private to you. The game only ever says how many entries exist.

**I am stuck.** Say `next`. If the answer does not help, ask your host.

## 8. Accessibility

* **Short text.** Every Selene reply is at most four short lines per page. Say `more` for the next page, so you never face a wall of text.
* **Plain words.** Each page ends with a line that starts "Next: say ..." so you always know a sensible next word.
* **No time pressure.** Nothing expires while you read, and nothing is a race.
* **Keys can be rebound** in Settings, then Controls. The Harr panel key (H) is rebindable too.
* **Dashboard.** Fully usable by keyboard, with skip links and visible focus. It works with screen readers
  (landmarks, labelled sections and a polite status line), follows your system's light or dark setting, and can be forced
  either way. Animations stop when your system asks for reduced motion. Text scales with browser zoom, and the page reflows from phone width up.
* **Print.** The print layout is black on white.
* If something is hard for you to use, tell your host: it is a bug in Harr, not in you.
