# Regional research lenses

Twenty fictional ecological districts now connect directly to related research proposals. Each has a small editorial SDG mapping based on its stated mayor focus. Region cards show that lens and an Explore related research action; mayor conversations also offer a route to the region’s research.

The Research desk has a regional lens selector alongside country, goal, status, institution and search filters. The server validates the region identifier, uses its mapped goal set and intersects the other filters before pagination. The response includes the lens name, focus, goal names and explicit qualification. Clearing the lens keeps other filters.

These are topic lenses, not geographic boundaries or assignments. They do not attach a country to a fictional region, declare a mayor’s nationality, rewrite mission ownership or generate/activate proposals. A country’s whole programme summary remains distinct from the filtered results. A named beneficiary, responsible owner, approved sources and independent review remain required for real execution.

For example, Farmlands maps to goals 2, 8 and 12. Nepal has eighteen matching seeded proposals, including six tea workflow templates, while its whole programme has seventy-four. These are backlog counts, not country findings or completed studies. Highlands and Farmlands can legitimately overlap on some questions; regional lenses do not partition the world’s research exclusively.

Verification checks all twenty mappings, frozen valid goal sets, composed country/goal/status filters, pagination, invalid identifiers, unchanged backlog/submission counts and unchanged instructions through mission activation. Public browser QA verifies the district-to-research route and country filtering.
