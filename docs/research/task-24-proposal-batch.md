## Concern model

Every generated country proposal belongs to exactly one concern. The country baseline collects country context and dated statistics. The three applied concerns are the product/destination-specific question, the institution mapping and the intervention comparison. A proposal that would be answered by the same evidence as another is the same proposal and is consolidated instead of emitted twice.

## Dedupe rule

Two candidate proposals are the same proposal when they share the same substantive key: <concern>|<theme-or-goal>|<country_code>. Baselines key on <country_code>|<goal> because a country baseline is answered by that country's own official statistics and is therefore distinct per country. Applied concerns key on <concern>|<theme>|<country_code>, so two goals that resolve to the same theme and concern for one country produce one row plus a recorded consolidation. The generator never emits the retired interchangeable stage ids (<iso2>-<goal>-evidence, -partners, -proposal): an applied concern is emitted only for a theme the catalog has scoped, and only where the theme's registered subject evidence exists for that country. A goal with no scoped theme keeps its baseline only; its applied concerns are recorded as open scope choices on that baseline.

## Workflow boundaries

Each proposal carries source prerequisites, dependencies, outputs and acceptance criteria (full text in the JSON). No proposal selects a beneficiary, activates a mission, establishes buyer demand, institutional partnership, funding or adoption, and no research finding is asserted.
