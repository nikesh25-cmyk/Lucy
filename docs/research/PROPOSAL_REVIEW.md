# Proposal review — 50 existing research proposals

Task 12 review of 50 seeded research proposals in `research_leads`, beginning with **agriculture market access** and **LDC bilateral cooperation**. This is an analysis and a proposed additive patch. It activates nothing, selects no real beneficiary and invents no completed research.

- **Batch:** 50 proposals, all read from a temporary database seeded by `src/civic-engine.js` `initCivic` (44 LDC catalog entries × 17 goals × 4 stages = 2,992 rows, plus 6 Nepal–India tea pilot leads = 2,998). Ids are deterministic, so every listed row exists in any seeded world. No live installation database, and nothing under `outputs/Lucy`, was read or written.
- **Themes:** the 6 Nepal–India tea pilot leads (agriculture market access), SDG 2 (Zero Hunger) country proposals (agriculture market access), SDG 17 (Partnerships for the Goals) country proposals (LDC bilateral cooperation).
- **Status:** every reviewed proposal stays `proposed`. A proposal may only move past `proposed` once an actual owner, beneficiary and independent reviewer are chosen.

## Classification counts

| classification | count | meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ready for human scoping | 11 | Question, scope, outputs, rubric, source categories, rights and dependencies are concrete enough to scope now. Nothing is activated. |
| useful proposal | 13 | Worthwhile, non-duplicate question with a real evidence route, but a prior scoping choice or an extra registered source is needed first. |

The non-duplicate useful set is 26 proposals (24 actionable + 2 blocked on absent evidence). The remaining 24 are interchangeable templates that should be consolidated rather than activated individually.

## Method and limits

- Each proposal was read from the seeded `research_leads` row (id, country_code, goal, stage, title, instructions, rubric, source_urls) and reviewed for its question, scope, beneficiary placeholder, prerequisites, outputs, acceptance rubric, source categories, rights and dependencies.
- `duplicate` rows were detected by exact instruction-template equality across the 44 LDC seeds at the same goal and stage: only the country name differs. The refinement restates what the row would need (a named commodity/instrument, value chain and destination) to become answerable, and recommends consolidation.
- `missing evidence` rests on catalog state, not judgement: Senegal’s three agriculture series are recorded as unavailable (`catalog/agriculture-statistics.json` → `metadata.unavailable`), and no retained source demonstrates buyer demand for `pilot-tea-5`.
- No live lead row was edited. The companion `docs/research/proposal-review-patch.json` is an additive patch keyed by proposal id; it never writes `status`, `mission_id` or `task_id`, and its `proposed_lead_patch` text is a proposal only.
- This review does not establish research correctness, buyer demand, institutional partnership, funding or country outcomes. Concrete full-detail refinements (scope, prerequisites, source categories, rights, dependencies) are in the JSON patch.
