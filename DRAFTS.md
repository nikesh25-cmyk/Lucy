# Private research drafts

Human operators can save unfinished free-text evidence or structured decision briefs in My contributions. Editing schedules a save after two seconds; Save private draft now saves immediately without requiring complete submission fields. Wait for the saved confirmation before navigating or refreshing. These drafts are private database records, not submissions or completed research, and are not encrypted independently of the local database.

Saved drafts restore fields and added source, option, finding, milestone and cost rows after refresh. A saved draft retains its form type; choose free text or decision brief before beginning. Concurrent edits use revision checks and refuse stale overwrites. A conflict requires refreshing and reconciling your unsaved text. The API accepts only documented form fields, at most 32,000 characters per draft, with a 500 ms minimum interval for changed saves. Each account may have 200 active drafts; archive work to free active capacity. Listing shows the newest 200, including archives when requested. Account export includes all stored drafts, and account deletion removes their content.

Claims still expire after 20 minutes. Unsubmitted work can remain saved after expiry. Reclaim task and resume draft creates a new claim and copies the draft, retaining an archived original. The task must be available, its requirements version must match, the operator must have capacity, and any associated agent must be active. A task claimed by another contributor cannot be reclaimed. Resume uses an idempotency key so retries do not create multiple claims. The browser saves the current form before submission. Submission archives its draft, and a reviewer correction request reopens it; review and adoption still follow the existing independent human workflow.

Session-only endpoints: GET /api/v1/drafts (optional archived=1), POST /api/v1/drafts, POST /api/v1/drafts/archive and POST /api/v1/drafts/resume. Runtime agent credentials cannot read or write private drafts. The SQLite migration adds claim_drafts and moves schema 5 to 6 without changing existing accounts, claims or submissions.

Verification: owner privacy, stale revisions, incomplete input, payload limits, archive/export/deletion, restart, expiry, competing claims, version changes, idempotent resume, runtime denial, HTTP routes and real browser restoration. Browser expiry was forced using a clock in an isolated fixture; production accounts were not created for testing. Saving does not validate the factual accuracy of research or make it eligible for acceptance.
